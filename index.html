Hi, I'm Emmy

A whole business
on a little stick.

Emmy's Sweet Treats started in 2019, in my kitchen in Enfield, with a single batch of birthday cake pops for my niece's first birthday. The order from her teacher came the next week. Then a wedding. Then a baby shower. Suddenly there was a business.

Every pop is still rolled by hand — no molds, no pre-mades. Whatever color you want, I can match it. Whatever theme, I can build around it. The only rule is: it has to taste like it was made for you, because it was.

— Emmy Baker · Owner · Sprinkle wrangler

Frequently
asked sweet things.

— ask me anything else Honest answers · no fluff
01 How far in advance should I place my order? +

At least one week for everyday orders, and three to four weeks for weddings or large events. Holidays like Mother's Day and Valentine's book up months ahead — get on the list early.

02 Do you deliver, or is it pickup only? +

Pickup is free from my Enfield kitchen — Fri evenings & Sat mornings by appointment. Local delivery within 10 miles is a flat $15. For weddings further out, message me; I've driven as far as Hartford and Springfield for the right pops.

03 What's the minimum order? +

A dozen (12) is the smallest order I take. Weddings have a 50-pop minimum. There's no maximum — the biggest single order so far was 320 for a corporate launch.

04 Can you match a specific color or theme? +

Almost always, yes. Send hex codes, swatch photos, or even your invitation and I'll match it. True neon and primary blue are the trickiest with cocoa butter — I'll let you know up front if I need to tweak the shade.

05 Do you do gluten-free or allergen-friendly? +

Gluten-free is available on request (no extra charge). My kitchen does work with peanuts, tree nuts, eggs, and dairy, so I can't guarantee zero cross-contact for severe allergies. Always mention allergies up front so I can give you an honest answer.

06 How long do they keep? +

Best within 3–5 days at room temperature, individually sleeved. Refrigerate for up to a week — bring back to room temp before serving so the chocolate doesn't sweat. They're not freezer-friendly with toppings.

07 How do I pay? +

A 50% deposit holds your date (Venmo, Zelle, or cash). Balance due at pickup. I'll send a quick confirmation note once the deposit lands so you know we're locked in.
